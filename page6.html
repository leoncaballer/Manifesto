Chapter Four

It doesn't have take long for a status quo to become ingrained. Coming back to the topic of AI, I already see how this technology has become a crutch for many people in their daily life. Now, it would be unthinkable for me to advocate for a ban on AI. There are simply too many great use cases for this technology to go to waste like that. In fact, I used AI to help me build the very website you are reading this on. However, besides the worrisome side effects I mentioned earlier, I find it concerning that AI is being used to replace human creativity and as an excuse for mass layoffs for the sake of "efficiency". In the near future, I want to learn even more about this technology, its implications, and, most importantly, how to regulate it. By doing this, I hope to be able to advocate against its negative aspects to keep them from crystallising into yet another harmful status quo, bringing together as many likeminded people as possible in the process. However, this doesn't end with me. Frankly, it doesn't end with the topic of AI either.
