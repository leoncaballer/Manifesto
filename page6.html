Changing AI

It is clear to see how the use of AI has already become a crutch for many people in their daily life. Despite its negative aspects, some companies and individuals are already advocating for the large-scale adoption of AI because it would bring about a new age of technology. Although this is not entirely wrong, I don't think it is wise to jump into this "revolution" so quickly. Of course, it would be unthinkable for me to advocate for a full ban on AI. There are simply too many great use cases for this technology to go to waste like that. In fact, I used a little bit of AI to help me build the very website you are reading this on. However, besides the worrisome side effects I mentioned earlier, I find it concerning that AI is being used to replace human creativity and as an excuse for mass layoffs for the sake of "efficiency". In addition, official regulation regarding AI is currently nowhere near up to date with current advancements. So, in the near future, I want to learn even more about this technology, its implications, and, most importantly, how to properly regulate it. By doing this, I hope to be able to advocate against its negative aspects to keep them from even crystallising into yet another harmful status quo (better safe than sorry!), bringing together as many likeminded people as possible in the process. I think it would be immensely sad to see this very promising technology go to waste because of chatbots and fake Picasso's.
