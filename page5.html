The Status Quo

Although they are certainly not comparable in every aspect, I think that both of the experiences you just read perfectly illustrate my message: it is never too late. Even after years have passed and the values and ideas you adhere to might feel ingrained, it is always possible to change course. Now, even though I am very proud of these choices, I am no superman. I think that everyone is able to do as I did, as long as they care enough. Of course, I am not telling you to throw everything you have established in your life out the window like a madman. But, if you have ever thought of making a change or taking a risk in your life and were held back by the comfort of the status quo, I am asking you to reconsider. Though not unbeatable, the status quo can be a powerful force, simply because it is easier to accept things the way they are, even if the way they are is harmful or unpleasant. Despite what my descriptions may portray it as, it isn't necessarily an evil force, it is simply the way things are right now. Whether it is harmful or not is entirely up to the people who shape it. Now, shaping the status quo might seem to be a task reserved for the rich and powerful, but when did the rich and powerful ever start a revolution? You can play a part in shaping it too! In fact, I think everybody can. Let me, for example, tell you about the formation of a brand new status quo and how I want to play a part in shaping it.
